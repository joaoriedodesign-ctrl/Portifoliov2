Building systems that help teams and products scale

I’m a Product Designer at Multibet, working on a portfolio of products for different markets. Before that, I was a UX Designer at Ana Gaming.

Design Systems, Design Ops and applied AI

My work goes from structure to interface: design systems that keep products consistent, processes that make teams faster and AI tools built into the workflow.

How I work

Design goes beyond aesthetics. Every visual decision has to answer a problem for the people who use the product and the people who build it.

Design Ops with AI: I use AI tools to speed up the repetitive parts (documentation, variations, reviews), leaving more time for decisions that need judgment.

Handoff is part of design. Clear specs, documented components and close conversation with engineering keep intent from getting lost in implementation.
