Context

Looking for a psychiatrist usually starts with doubt and hesitation. The landing page had to help visitors recognize themselves in what they read, build trust and offer one clear next step.

Design decisions

  1. 01

    Starting with recognition

    The page opens with what the visitor is going through, so they recognize themselves before any introduction of the practice.

  2. 02

    A single conversion channel

    The whole journey leads to the same place: WhatsApp. No competing forms or parallel paths.

  3. 03

    Trust before the click

    The information that supports the decision appears before the request to get in touch.

  4. 04

    Light and accessible

    Lean code with no frameworks, with accessibility treated as a requirement from the start.
